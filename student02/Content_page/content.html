What's GLOBAL WARMING ?

Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth’s average surface temperature due to human activities, primarily the release of greenhouse gases like
carbon dioxide (CO₂) and methane (CH₄) from burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes. This warming leads to climate changes, including rising sea levels,
extreme weather events, and disruptions to ecosystems.

How we can REDUCE the GLOBAL WARMING ?

We can reduce global warming by adopting sustainable practices that lower greenhouse gas emissions. Switching to renewable
energy sources like solar and wind power reduces reliance on fossil fuels,which are the main contributors to climate change.
Energy efficiency in homes, industries, and transportation, such as using LED lights, electric vehicles, and public transport,
also helps decrease carbon emissions.Planting trees and protecting forests absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere, while reducing
waste and promoting recycling lower methane emissions from landfills. Governments and individuals can also support policies
and lifestyle changes that prioritize sustainability, ensuring a healthier planet for future generations.

Is GLOBAL WARMING affects everyone ?

Global warming affects everyone, but its impacts are more severe for vulnerable communities, wildlife, and future generations.
Low-income and coastal communities face rising sea levels, extreme weather events, and food shortages, making it harder to adapt.
Farmers struggle with unpredictable weather, droughts, and floods, which threaten food production. Wildlife experiences habitat loss,
leading to species extinction and ecosystem imbalances. Additionally, future generations will inherit a planet with more severe climate
challenges if global warming is not addressed. The effects of climate change are widespread, making it crucial for everyone to take action to reduce its impact.

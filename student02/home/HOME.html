WHAT HAPPEND TO OUR WORLD?

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperature, weather patterns, and environmental conditions on Earth, primarily driven
by human activities such as
burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes. These actions release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide (CO₂) and methane (CH₄) into the
atmosphere, trapping heat and causing global temperatures to rise—a phenomenon known as global warming.



IS THERE ANY SOLUTION FOR THIS?

One of the most effective solutions for climate change is the active involvement of volunteer programs that focus on environmental protection
and sustainability.These programs empower individuals to take direct action by participating in tree-planting initiatives, coastal and urban
clean-ups, wildlife conservation efforts, and educational campaigns that promote sustainable living. Volunteers also contribute to climate advocacy,
pushing for stronger environmental policies and raising awareness in their communities.By joining these programs,
people can help reduce carbon emissions, restore ecosystems, and promote renewable energy solutions.
Collective action through volunteering not only mitigatesclimate change but also inspires long-term sustainable practices
that benefit both the environment and future generations.
